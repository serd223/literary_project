Highschool in general has a lot of core memories for me. From the second I
started ninth grade, i hated everyhing about me. The way I looked, the way I talked, the
way I thought, the way I acted... Every single thing about me was wrong. And its shocking
to a lot of people in my life because I always had this act going on about knowing who I
was and who I wanted to be. But the truth was, I had no idea about who I was, especially
about who I wanted to be. That part of my life was very diffucult. And for my family,
where everybody knew who they were, I was so out of place. They made me feel like I
was ungrateful for being like this. I was lost at home, at school, at my dorm, in any place
you can think of. Nobody knowed who I was including me. It was like a movie where
every scene was directed by someone else.
