So I did what any wise person would do. I literally collected personality traits. First
I questioned what would make me unique (mind you I was a kid). And made the
resolutions mine, sort of. Biggest example is the color yellow. I always had a obsession
with yellow. Everything about it made me feel safe. When I learned that only %3 of the
people in the world’s favorite color was yellow, it clicked to me. I HAD A UNIQUE TRAIT.
Don’t get me wrong I am not being performative about yellow. I am pretty sure a lot of
people love yellow now. But it was one of the rare moments that made me feel special. I
know it sounds stupid for a lot of reasons but anything after this went downhill .
