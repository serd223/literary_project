Growing up in my family was a weird experience. Every person in our isolated
family has a designated role. My dad is the bread winner and the good cop in parental
situations. My mom is the stone cold, serious and bit of a scary leader. My aunt, who
lives next door is religious and she values spirituality more than anything. And my older
sister is the cool angsty teen everybody wanted to be at some part of their lives (at least I
did.) Everybody knew their place and what they wanted to be. Not just family to be exact.
İn my school, in my neighbourhood, everybody was someone. When it comes to me
things get complicated. There is a quote from one of my favorite movies of all time, Mary
and Max. Max says “Growing up I wanted to be anybody but myself” and it stuck with me.
There were a lot of weird phases in my life. I tried to be like my sister,my couisins that I
grew up with, my cool friends at school, random people on internet, those aesthetic girls
at pinterest, especially characters in the movies I grew up with. And none of them was
the right fit for me. Well in theory it was but something always felt off. I never truly liked
any of them, because the feeling of being nobody corrupted my brain.
