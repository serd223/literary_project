Now, I am in that infamous “in-between.” Every morning I open my computer to forms, exam
dates, well-meaning wishes of “I wish it all turn out well”… Outside, the world keeps
spinning, everyone seems to be rushing somewhere. And I stand at the very center of
uncertainty. As I try to fit myself into the system out there, I feel myself slowly losing the
person within. The “me” outside has to succeed, has to become something respectable. But at
night, when my head touches the pillow, that same question lingers:
Is this person I’ve become truly me—or just a stranger shaped by the noise of the outside
world?
