And then, in a single day, the mist dissolved. The caps were thrown into the air,
congratulations were exchanged, and that word I had trusted so much—“then”—stepped
forward and stood right in front of me. There was no midterm week left to hide behind, no
“let me just finish this assignment” to cling to. When I took off the robe and set it aside, I
realized that the title of “student” had slipped away from me as well—and I didn’t even know
what remained in its place.
