I feel myself coming to peace. In my ears, a familiar melody echoes: “Dön bak dünyaya”
(Turn and look at the world) (Pinhani). This new landscape no longer frightens me. On the
contrary, stripped of all the labels that once defined me, I begin to ask: Is my life only just
beginning? Perhaps now it is time to set out in search for “me” that has ben silently waiting,
somewhere deep inside.
