The composers of my concerto are lost; who has stolen the heteronyms within me? We had said that growing up would multiply them, yet with each passing day I gnaw away at all the hopes and aspirations I’ve lost. Sometimes I want to create a revolution in my heart; let the piece’s dominant language be the piano, not the violin. If the preface of my frozen blood flows through the doors I open, then perhaps we can await the seasons, just like Vivaldi.
Moving forward is hard; sometimes even the war inside me pushes me backward. Fighting on the fronts God has opened is very hard; my thoughts can only confront one thing at a time. I love placing the verb at the end of the sentence; it feels like it completes my orchestra. Shields may slip from my hands in battle, and sometimes, in pursuit of my goal, secondary factors are forgotten. Maybe I’m not as smart as I used to be; my narcissistic personality no longer even sees itself as a person. I burned myself the most in those fiery flames; it’s so hard to remember the old me. The detective showed me the evidence in my case; neither the killer nor the victim can be found.
I struggle even to write a simple sentence; believe me, the rhyme at the end isn’t intentional. The music in my ear won’t stop; even if you ring my left ear, you still can’t escape the revolution raging inside me. My soul is drunk; alcohol’s only effect is letting the world digest the tears in my body. I think there’s no antidote for this illness; you must leave me somewhere between Scylla and Charybdis. My illness awaits my 27th year; I’ve sworn to exhale my tainted breath one last time.
My captive soul will never break its chains; I have no teeth left to shatter them. My strength refuses to surrender its will to me; I lack the courage to bear it. The arrows of my feelings were launched into the air, soared to the heavens, and fell to the earth. My ascents in life have faded like a fire; no one poured water on them, yet they left a trail behind, and the sickle was never cast into the field again. Do not plant any sapling or seed in my farm; there is no gardener to tend this garden. Do not trust the old daffodils you sniffed in December; as you see, every flower is far from blooming.
Santa Claus has come with his lights; how happy I am, isn’t it? I should be sparkling! I can’t open my eyes to look around; as if the sun has fallen asleep on purpose, leaving only me behind. I’m unaware of what’s happening; the country is falling apart, yet I’m fixated on magazine covers! People have spilled into the streets; I must realize the cause isn’t kindness, but fanaticism. My own ideals are an idol to me; I must be a barrier against those who shatter them. In the end, everyone loses; as long as I’m the only one who wins! (!)
They’re cutting down the trees of my entire forest; I don’t want the wood you take from my greenery. I stood up without flinching; the cowards watch the cursers from the window. No one knows the truth; the tinted windows seem like the highest curtain. I must worship the king; oh, may his crown not fall from the highest sanctuary! I must die in my own pit; let them lay my shroud as if it were nothing. I must watch the next episode of my series there; the harmony between me and an ordinary citizen continues.
What must I do to move forward? The steps I take can’t take me any further. I say humanity must shatter; the sharpest thorn of my smile no longer hurts. I endure people; perhaps I’ve become the scarecrow in their gardens. They never change; the machine they’ve placed has produced their chips exactly as programmed. Money, love, fame; is this the deepest part of the ocean in all your dreams? Am I living in vain; I can’t reveal the shadow of my iceberg to anyone.
There are some writers who fit you into their novels; their silhouettes are usually the physical form of who I am. In the steps I take, I feel their sentences; I always imagine myself as someone else. I am the only one who knows myself in this world; the portrait I see in the mirror is not me. Dorian Gray lives within me; an aged painting. That is why the skin of those who touch me trembles; a trace of my brush remains on them. The fifth act of my opera is coming; please do not lose your focus.
The colors of my orchestra are gray; I strike my instruments with my harshest tone, living in a dream. The killer within me sees only the deaths of those he loves; he ignites the fires of hell with the tears in his eyes. The sound of the notes rises; I find myself lost in every moment at the ball. It’s as if I’m the only one who doesn’t see the masks; everyone has hidden their ugly faces beneath them. Everyone keeps time with my rhythm; I notice their ingratitude in their dances. They are whatever the music is; I thought I was different, but I wasn’t. Violin, piano, cello, harp—all lies; bring me the next nightmare now.
