Welcome to Echoes Between Strangers

This project consists of a virtual book that has its chapters written by different authors who vary in age and experience. These authors came together to express their struggles and make you feel seen. In order to read all the chapters, you have to receive each chapter from another reader by scanning their generated QR code. You are not allowed to receive more than one chapter from the same device. When you transmit a chapter, you give access to the chapter following the one you are currently reading to a different reader near you. In this way, you can share your mental experiences and sufferings with a person you have just met or deepen the relationships you already have. The aim of the project is for you to find and interact with people that have been sharing the same thoughts and concerns as you. Remember, you are not alone in this fight inside you. Hope this book helps you get through things.
PS: Beware, some of the contents might be triggering for you.


Creator: Elifnur Bölükbaş

“Elifnur Bölükbaş, born in 2006, lives in Ankara and has graduated from Prof. Dr. Fuat Sezgin Science High School. She is currently a student of American Culture and Literature at Hacettepe University. Her academic interests include literary analysis, art history, and historical studies, and painting as a form of visual expression. In this project, Elifnur develops a virtual, collaborative book in which chapters are written by different authors of varying ages and experiences, coming together to express personal struggles and foster a sense of being understood. The reading process is designed as an interactive experience: readers must obtain each chapter by scanning QR codes from others, and each shared chapter unlocks the next for another nearby reader. Through this system, the project encourages interpersonal interaction, aiming to address feelings of isolation, lack of empathy, and difficulty in forming connections in the modern digital world, while creating a shared space for emotional exchange and mutual understanding.”

“Serdar Çoruhlu, born in 2007 in Istanbul, is the web developer of this project. He is currently studying Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering at Istanbul Technical University.”
